
# 🍬 US Candy Distribution Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

### 1. Project Title / Headline

🍭 **Sweet Insights: US Candy Sales & Distribution Dashboard**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze candy sales, gross profit, product performance, and factory-level distribution across the United States. The dashboard provides a visual overview of total sales, total profit, units sold, and profit margin, helping users explore sales trends and compare the profitability of different candy products.

### 2. Short Description / Purpose

The US Candy Distribution Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report developed to analyze candy sales performance and profitability across products and factories. It provides key performance indicators such as total sales, total profit, total units sold, and profit margin, along with visualizations that highlight annual sales trends and product-level profit contribution.

The dashboard includes a factory selection slicer that allows users to filter the analysis by factory, making it easier to explore performance across different distribution locations. It is designed for business analysts, sales managers, and decision-makers interested in understanding product profitability and monitoring candy sales performance.

### 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main data visualization platform used to develop the interactive dashboard.
- 📂 **Power Query** – Used for data preparation, transformation, and cleaning before analysis.
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Supports analytical calculations and measures for sales, profit, units sold, and profitability.
- 🗂️ **Data Modeling** – Uses sales, product, and factory-related tables to support product-level and factory-level analysis.
- 📈 **Interactive Visualizations** – KPI cards, column charts, pie charts, gauge visuals, and image-based dashboard elements.
- 🎛️ **Power BI Slicers** – Factory selection filter for exploring sales performance across different factories.
- 🔖 **Bookmarks and Buttons** – Includes a reset button to support dashboard navigation and restore the report's saved filter state.
- 🎨 **Dashboard Design** – Custom background imagery, themed visuals, and layout elements create a candy-inspired reporting experience.
- 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for the Power BI project and `.png` for dashboard screenshots and previews.

### 4. Data Source

**Source: US Candy Distribution Dataset**

The dashboard uses structured candy sales data organized around sales transactions, candy products, and factories.

The data model includes the following analytical areas:

- **Sales Data (`Candy_Sales`)** – Sales amounts, gross profit, units sold, and order dates.
- **Product Data (`Candy_Products`)** – Candy product names used to compare product-level profitability.
- **Factory Data (`Candy_Factories`)** – Factory information used to filter and analyze sales performance by factory.
- **Date Information** – Order dates used to analyze annual sales trends.
- **Sales Metrics** – Total sales, total profit, and total units sold.
- **Profitability Metrics** – Gross profit and profit margin used to evaluate financial performance.

These data elements enable users to analyze sales trends, compare product profitability, and investigate performance using the available factory filter.

### 5. Features / Highlights

**• Business Problem**

Candy distribution businesses need to monitor sales, profitability, product performance, and factory-level results to understand which products contribute most to revenue and profit.

Analyzing raw sales records can make it difficult to identify annual trends, compare product profitability, and investigate differences between factories.

Important business questions include:

- What are the total sales and gross profit generated by the business?
- How many candy units have been sold?
- How does sales performance change from year to year?
- Which candy products contribute the most to total profit?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- How does performance change when a different factory is selected?

The dashboard addresses these questions by consolidating key business metrics and product-level analysis into an interactive Power BI report.

**• Goal of the Dashboard**

To develop an interactive business intelligence solution that:

- Provides a centralized view of sales and profitability KPIs.
- Tracks total sales, gross profit, and units sold.
- Analyzes annual sales trends.
- Compares profit contribution across candy products.
- Displays profit margin through a gauge visualization.
- Enables factory-level filtering to investigate performance.
- Supports data-driven decisions related to product performance and distribution.

**• Walkthrough of Key Visuals**

- **Total Sales (KPI Card)**

  Displays total sales revenue generated across the available candy sales data. This metric provides a quick overview of overall sales performance and responds to the selected factory filter.

- **Total Profit (KPI Card)**

  Shows the total gross profit generated by candy sales, helping users understand the overall profitability of the selected data.

- **Total Units (KPI Card)**

  Displays the total number of candy units sold, providing an overview of sales volume.

- **Total Sales by Year (Column Chart)**

  Visualizes sales performance across years using order date information. This chart helps users identify annual sales patterns and compare performance across reporting periods.

- **Total Profit by Product Name (Pie Chart)**

  Breaks down gross profit by candy product, helping users compare product-level contributions to total profit.

  The visualization includes products such as Wonka Bar - Fudge Mallows and Wonka Bar - Nutty Crunch Surprise, allowing users to explore how different products contribute to profitability.

- **Profit Margin (Gauge)**

  Provides a gauge-style visualization associated with the gross profit measure. This visual offers a compact view of profitability performance.

  *Note: Verify the gauge's value and formatting settings in Power BI before interpreting it as a percentage-based profit margin.*

- **Select Factory (Slicer)**

  Allows users to select a factory and filter the report's analytical visuals. This supports factory-level comparisons of sales, profit, units, and product performance.

- **Reset Button (Bookmark)**

  Provides a convenient way to restore the report's saved bookmark state and reset the dashboard view, depending on the configured bookmark settings.

- **Custom Dashboard Background**

  Uses candy-themed background imagery and visual styling to create a distinctive dashboard experience that aligns with the subject of the analysis.

**• Business Impact & Insights**

- **Sales Performance Monitoring:** Track total sales and units sold to understand overall business performance.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Monitor gross profit and investigate which products contribute most to business profitability.
- **Product Performance:** Compare candy products by their contribution to total profit.
- **Annual Trend Analysis:** Analyze sales performance across years to identify changes in revenue over time.
- **Factory-Level Analysis:** Use the factory slicer to explore performance across different distribution locations.
- **Business Decision Support:** Consolidate important sales and profitability indicators into a single interactive report.
- **Data Visualization Skills:** Demonstrate practical use of Power BI, data modeling, KPI cards, chart design, slicers, and bookmarks.
